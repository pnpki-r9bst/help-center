Sign Documents Using Digital Signatures

Purpose​

This guide explains how to sign electronic and digital documents using digital signatures with PNPKI. It aims to help users securely authenticate and protect their documents, ensuring integrity, authenticity, and compliance with digital signature standards.

Procedure​

What You Need:
  1. Desktop or laptop
  2. Digital Certificate (.p12 file) installed on device
  3. Application with digital certificate signing capabilities (e.g. Adobe Reader & Foxit Reader)
  4. Electronic Document in .pdf format
  • ADOBE READER

Step 1: Open a .pdf document with Adobe Acrobat Reader

Step 2: Go to All Tools -> View More and click on Use a Certificate

Step 3: Choose Digitally sign then click and drag to create a rectangle shape where the signature will be set

Step 4: Select the digital ID which was issued by Digital Signing CA and click Continue

Step 5: Click on Sign and save the document to place the digital signature.

FAQs​

Do I need an internet connection to sign documents?

No, signing of documents using PNPKI digital certificates does not require internet connection.

I can't see my digital certificate when trying to sign.

Make sure the digital certificate is installed on the device before trying to sign documents. Please see how to install digital certificates for more info.
