Apply for an Agency Digital Certificate

Purpose​

This guide aims to assist PNPKI individual applicants in successfully applying for a digital certificate by providing clear and detailed instructions. It ensures that applicants understand the requirements and steps needed to complete the application process efficiently and in compliance with PNPKI policies.

NOTE:

Agency Certificates are digital certificates that represent the entire organization or government agency.

Procedure​

  • ONLINE
  • WALK-IN
What You Need:
  1. Device - Desktop, laptop or mobile phone with internet access
  2. Email – Valid email of the organization or authorized representative
  3. Authorized Representative's Documents:
    • PhilID/ePhilID - Softcopy (JPG, PNG, or PDF)
    • Photo – Passport-size (JPG or PNG)
  4. Agency Documents:
    • Tax Identification Number (TIN)
    • Authorization Letter or Board Resolution designating the representative - (PDF)
  5. Additional Requirements:
    • For Government Agencies:
      • GSIS Registration Number
    • For Non-Government Entities:
      • SEC Registration (for corporations/partnerships)
      • DTI Registration (for sole proprietors)
      • CDA Registration (for cooperatives)
      • Business Permit (from LGU)
      • SSS Employer Clearance

Step 1: Fill out the PNPKI Online Registration System (ORS) form and upload the required documents.

Step 2: Check your email regularly for evaluation results and your identity verification schedule.

Step 3: Attend identity verification (online or face-to-face) and bring the original copies of your uploaded documents.

Step 4: Once approved, check your email for download instructions and install your digital certificate.

FAQs​

I have no Philippine National ID. What should I do?

Prepare the following:

  1. Birth Certificate or Passport

  2. Unified Multi-Purpose ID (UMID)

    I don't have a UMID. What can I use?

    Prepare two (2) valid government-issued IDs from the list:

    • Driver’s License (LTO)
    • PRC ID
    • NBI Clearance
    • Police Clearance
    • Postal ID
    • COMELEC Voter’s ID
    • GSIS e-Card
    • SSS Card
    • Senior Citizen Card
    • OFW ID / OWWA ID
    • Seaman’s Book
    • Alien Certification of Registration
    • Government Office & GOCC ID (e.g., AFP ID, HDMF ID)
    • DSWD Certification
    • IBP ID
    • Company ID (issued by SEC, BSP, or Insurance Commission-regulated companies)
How long does it take to process an agency digital certificate?

Online applications usually take 3 days, while walk-in applications may be processed faster, depending on verification schedules.

Can an agency apply for multiple digital certificates?

No, only one certificate is issued per agency or organization. However, regional offices, branches, or divisions (e.g., DICT Region 9) may apply separately, but their certificate will represent only their specific region/office and not the entire agency.

Who can be the authorized representative?

Anyone officially designated by the agency via an Authorization Letter or Board Resolution, typically an employee handling digital transactions.

Can I use my personal email for an agency certificate?

No, it's recommended to use the agency’s official email to ensure continuous access and proper management of digital certificates.

What if the authorized representative resigns or changes?

The agency must apply for a certificate revocation and reapply with a newly authorized representative.

Do I need to bring original documents for identity verification?

Yes, the authorized representative must bring original copies of all submitted documents for verification.

Can a third-party service provider apply on behalf of an agency?

No, only officially designated representatives authorized by the agency can apply.

What happens if I don’t receive a confirmation email?

Check your spam/junk folder. If you still haven’t received it within 3 days, contact the PNPKI support team at r9.pnpki@dict.gov.ph.
