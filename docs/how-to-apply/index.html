Apply for an Individual Digital Certificate

Purpose​

This guide aims to assist PNPKI individual applicants in successfully applying for a digital certificate by providing clear and detailed instructions. It ensures that applicants understand the requirements and steps needed to complete the application process efficiently and in compliance with PNPKI policies.

note

To apply on behalf of your organization or agency, click here

Procedure​

  • ONLINE
  • WALK-IN
What You Need:
  1. Device – Desktop, laptop, or mobile phone with internet access
  2. Email – Active and accessible
  3. Tax Identification Number (TIN)
  4. PhilID/ePhilID - Softcopy (JPG, PNG, or PDF)
  5. Photo – Passport-size (JPG or PNG)

Step 1: Fill out the PNPKI Online Registration System (ORS) form and upload the required documents.

Step 2: Check your email regularly for evaluation results and your identity verification schedule.

Step 3: Attend identity verification (online or face-to-face) and bring the original copies of your uploaded documents.

Step 4: Once approved, check your email for download instructions and install your digital certificate.

What You Need:
  1. Email – Active and accessible
  2. Tax Identification Number (TIN)
  3. PhilID/ePhilID - Original & Photocopy
  4. Photo – Passport-size

Step 1: Fill out the PNPKI Application Form (Available at the office or online).

Step 2: Undergo identity verification (Bring original documents).

Step 3: Check your email for download instructions and install your digital certificate.

FAQs​

I have no Philippine National ID. What should I do?

Prepare the following:

  1. Birth Certificate or Passport

  2. Unified Multi-Purpose ID (UMID)

    I don't have a UMID. What can I use?

    Prepare two (2) valid government-issued IDs from the list:

    • Driver’s License (LTO)
    • PRC ID
    • NBI Clearance
    • Police Clearance
    • Postal ID
    • COMELEC Voter’s ID
    • GSIS e-Card
    • SSS Card
    • Senior Citizen Card
    • OFW ID / OWWA ID
    • Seaman’s Book
    • Alien Certification of Registration
    • Government Office & GOCC ID (e.g., AFP ID, HDMF ID)
    • DSWD Certification
    • IBP ID
    • Company ID (issued by SEC, BSP, or Insurance Commission-regulated companies)
How long does it take to process my PNPKI application?

Online applications usually take 3 days, while walk-in applications may be processed faster, depending on verification schedules.

What happens if I don’t receive a confirmation email?

Check your spam/junk folder. If you still haven’t received it within 3 days, contact the PNPKI support team at r9.pnpki@dict.gov.ph.

Can I apply on behalf of someone else?

No, PNPKI applications require personal identity verification.

Can I use a company email instead of a personal email?

Yes, but it’s recommended to use a personal email to ensure uninterrupted access to your PNPKI account.

Can I apply without a TIN?

No, a Tax Identification Number (TIN) is a mandatory requirement.
