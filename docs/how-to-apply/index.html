Apply for an Individual Digital Certificate

Purpose​

This guide aims to assist PNPKI individual applicants in successfully applying for a digital certificate by providing clear and detailed instructions. It ensures that applicants understand the requirements and steps needed to complete the application process efficiently and in compliance with PNPKI policies.

note

To apply on behalf of your organization or agency, click here

Procedure​

  • ONLINE
  • WALK-IN
What You Need:
  1. Desktop or Laptop or Mobile Phone
  2. Valid Email Address and Tax Identification Number (TIN)
  3. Softcopy of Philippine National ID (PhilID or ePhilID)
  4. Softcopy of passport size photo.
  5. Internet connection.

Step 1: Accomplish the form through the Online Registration System (ORS).

Step 2: Regularly check inbox for evaluation results and identity verification appointment from the PNPKI team.

Step 3: Undergo the identity verification either through online or face-to-face.

Step 4: Regularly check inbox for the email from the PNPKI system to download your digital certificate.

What You Need:
  1. Valid Email Address and Tax Identification Number (TIN)
  2. Philippine National ID (PhilID or ePhilID)
  3. Passport size photo.

Step 1: Accomplish the Fillable PNPKI Application Form.

Step 2: Undergo the identity verification.

Step 3: Regularly check inbox for the email from the PNPKI system to download your digital certificate.

FAQs​

I have no Philippine National ID.

Prepare the following:

  1. Birth Certificate or Passport

  2. UMID

    No UMID?

    Prepare 2 of any of the accepted IDs on the list:

    • LTO Driver’s License
    • Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) ID
    • National Bureau of Investigation (NBI) Clearance
    • Police Clearance Certificate
    • Postal Identity Card
    • COMELEC Voter’s ID
    • Government Service Insurance System (GSIS) e-Card
    • Social Security System (SSS) Card
    • OSCA Senior Citizen Card
    • Overseas Workers Welfare Administration (OWWA) ID / Overseas Filipino Worker (OFW) ID
    • Seaman’s Book
    • Alien Certification of Registration/Immigrant Certificate of Registration
    • Government Office and GOCC ID, e.g. Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP ID), Home Development Mutual Fund (HDMF ID)
    • Certification from the National Council for the Welfare of Disabled Persons (NCWDP)
    • Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) Certification
    • Integrated Bar of the Philippines ID
    • Company IDs Issued by Private Entities or Institutions Registered with or Supervised or Regulated either by the BSP, SEC, or Insurance Commission (IC)
